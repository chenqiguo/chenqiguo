## 🚀 Selected Projects

### RethinkKD — Understanding Attention, Fidelity, and Generalization in Knowledge Distillation
**Expert Systems with Applications, 2025 · First & Corresponding Author**

[Code](https://github.com/zisci2/RethinkKD)

A systematic study of why knowledge distillation works, investigating the relationships among teacher attention diversity, student–teacher fidelity, mutual information, and data augmentation. Experiments cover ResNet, VGG, and ViT architectures on CIFAR100, ImageNet, and their long-tailed variants.

---

### PCB — Physical Commonsense Benchmark for Generated Videos
**Ongoing Research**

A benchmark and automatic evaluation framework for assessing the physical plausibility of generated videos.

The project integrates **video object tracking and segmentation, structured physical-state extraction, LLM-based equation discovery, physical-law fitting, and human-aligned evaluation**, with real videos, generated videos, and controllable physics perturbations.

---

### SpeLER — Spectral-Balanced Energy Propagation
**ICCV 2025 · Co-first Author**

[Code](https://github.com/ydanwang/SpeLER)

A spectral perspective on skeleton-based action recognition under class imbalance and partial labeling. The method combines spectral-balanced label propagation, propagation-energy-based reliability estimation, and temporal/frequency representations.

---

### Fully Automatic Breast Cosmesis Assessment
**Machine Learning with Applications, 2022 · First Author**

[Project & Code](https://github.com/chenqiguo/fullyAutomaticBreastCosmesisSystem)

An end-to-end computer vision and machine learning system for automatically evaluating cosmetic outcomes after breast-conserving therapy, developed through collaboration with radiation oncologists from multiple U.S. medical institutions.

The system includes image preprocessing, breast/areola/nipple detection, landmark estimation, clinical feature extraction, and multidimensional outcome prediction.

---

### When Do GANs Replicate?
**ICCV 2021 · Co-first Author**

[Code](https://github.com/chenqiguo/GAN_replication)

A systematic investigation of training-data replication and memorization in BigGAN and StyleGAN2. We studied how replication changes with dataset size and complexity and developed a one-shot estimator for predicting replication behavior.
